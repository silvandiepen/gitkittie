## Rewriting history

GitKittie Bud can rewrite git history — rebasing, squashing, reordering, and removing files from past commits — and can push those rewrites to your remote. These operations change history that other people may already have, and force-pushing can discard work. The Software previews rewrites and creates a safety reference before running them, but you remain responsible for what you confirm and push, and for coordinating with anyone else working in the repository.

## Sending, sorting and cleaning up mail

GitKittie Mail hands outgoing mail to your provider and shows you what it knows about each send. It cannot guarantee that a message is delivered, or when, because delivery is up to your provider and the recipient's. When a send's outcome is unclear, the app does not send it again on its own; it is up to you to decide.

Sorting mail into Focused and other views is done by rules and can be wrong. Check the other views for mail you expect. Sorting never moves or deletes mail at your provider by itself. Actions that do — archiving, moving, deleting, sender rules that archive, and cleanup batches — run only when you confirm them, show what they will change first, and can be undone in the app; deleting from Trash and actions your provider carries out on its own may not be undoable.

Unsubscribing sends a request to the sender of a mailing list. Whether the sender honours it is up to them.
