# MAIL-100: A deliberate selection is acted on as chosen

**Decision.** A deliberate selection is acted on as chosen: Clean Up's protections (MAIL-059: flagged, spaces, recent, receipts…) are for what Clean Up suggests, not for what the person selected. The safety rules of the actions themselves still hold: Delete Permanently only from Trash and after its confirmation, nothing expunged from All Mail (MAIL-036), the provider's state wins a conflict (MAIL-035). Block and Always Junk are not offered here: they are a rule kind of their own (GKM-072, MAIL-109..111), offered in the row menu, not on a selection

**Why.** The person picked these rows; second-guessing a selection makes the tool unpredictable, and Undo covers mistakes. GKM-071
