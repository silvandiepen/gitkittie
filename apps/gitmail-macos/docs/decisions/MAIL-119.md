# MAIL-119: Where it lives

**Decision.** Where it lives: Mail ▸ Settings… ▸ **Organisation** on the Mac and Settings ▸ **Organisation** on iPhone and iPad (per account: the switch, which device runs it and why it waits, a row per policy with its age, its action and Preview & Turn On…; the dry run sheet; the History with Undo). Move to Trash says that the provider may empty its Trash by itself. **Not built**: active mode for Block (Always Junk, Always Trash, GKM-072, which moves mail by design and is asked in review GKM-031-organisation), and retention of verification codes and spam (MAIL-115)

**Why.** Not yet run against Gmail or iCloud (GKM-004). No macOS UI tests (the views were not driven). GKM-031
