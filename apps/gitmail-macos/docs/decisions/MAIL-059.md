# MAIL-059: Protected mail is never part of a batch

**Decision.** Protected mail is never part of a batch: flagged; in a space; uncertain (no verdict, a verdict from an older rule set, purpose unknown, risk suspicious, or confidence under 0.7 unless you decided it); quarantined; Focused, conversation or security mail; receipts, orders, invoices and bookings unless you tick "Include receipts"; a conversation with a message in the last 14 days; a conversation you wrote in (your address, the Sent folder, or `\Answered` at the provider). The preview lists protected messages with their reasons

**Why.** ThreatModel §12; Classification.md, Retention. Pinned mail does not exist yet: flagged stands for it. GKM-021
